# Workflow: Export Health Predisposition Reports

1. Navigate to `https://you.23andme.com/reports/health_predispositions/`.
2. Inspect cards for reported variants (e.g. APOE e4, BRCA1/BRCA2, Parkinson's G2019S).
3. Extract status text, condition name, and risk level.
4. Format into health workspace observations schema.
